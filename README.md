# LearnPi - Online Learning Platform

## About LearnPi

**LearnPi** is an interactive online educational platform designed to make learning fun and engaging. The name combines "Learn" with "π" (Pi) - symbolizing the joy of learning mathematics, science, and more!

Our mission is to provide quality education with a focus on making complex concepts simple and enjoyable for students at all levels.

## What is This Website?

LearnPi is an educational platform that offers:

- **Interactive Courses** - Structured learning paths for students
- **Multiple Subjects** - Focus on Mathematics, Physics, and Science
- **Class-Specific Content** - Tailored curriculum for different grade levels (including Class 9)
- **Study Resources** - Comprehensive learning materials and chapter-wise resources
- **Expert Faculty** - Learn from experienced educators
- **Affordable Pricing** - Flexible subscription plans with early bird offers
